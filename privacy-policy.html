Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 9, 2025

TL;DR: Movie to GIF does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. All video processing happens locally on your device. Your videos and GIFs remain completely private.

Introduction

Welcome to Movie to GIF. This Privacy Policy explains how our iOS application handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

By using Movie to GIF, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Movie to GIF is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Your videos or video content
  • Generated GIF files
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or statistics
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Any other personal or non-personal information

How the App Works

Movie to GIF operates entirely on your device:

  • Local Processing: All video-to-GIF conversion happens locally on your iPhone or iPad
  • No Internet Required: The app does not require an internet connection to function
  • No Cloud Storage: Your videos and GIFs are never uploaded to any server
  • No Third-Party Services: We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services

Permissions We Request

Movie to GIF requests the following permissions to function properly:

Photo Library Access

Why we need it: To allow you to select videos from your photo library for conversion and to save generated GIFs back to your photo library.

What we do with it: We only access the specific videos you choose to convert. We do not scan, index, or access any other content in your photo library.

File System Access (Optional)

Why we need it: To allow you to import videos from Files app or other locations.

What we do with it: We only access files you explicitly select through the file picker.

Data Storage

All data related to your use of Movie to GIF is stored locally on your device:

  • App Settings: Your preferences (quality settings, frame rate, etc.) are stored locally using iOS UserDefaults
  • Temporary Files: During conversion, temporary files may be created in the app's sandbox. These are automatically deleted when no longer needed
  • No Cloud Sync: Settings and data are not synced across devices or backed up to any cloud service

Third-Party Services

Movie to GIF does not integrate with any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics platforms (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media platforms
  • Cloud storage services
  • Crash reporting tools

The app operates completely independently and does not communicate with any external servers.

Children's Privacy

Movie to GIF does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else. The app is suitable for all ages and does not contain any age-restricted content or features.

Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of inadvertently collecting information from children under 13 (or any other age group).

Data Security

Since Movie to GIF does not collect or transmit any data, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to your information through our app.

Your videos and GIFs are protected by:

  • iOS sandbox security - the app can only access files you explicitly grant permission to
  • Device-level encryption provided by iOS
  • Your device's passcode/biometric authentication

Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data to:

  • Access or request
  • Correct or update
  • Delete or erase
  • Export or transfer

You maintain complete control over your videos and GIFs at all times through your device's photo library and file system.

If we ever change our practices to collect data (which we have no plans to do), we will:
